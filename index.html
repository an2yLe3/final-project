What is BJJ

01 CONTROL

Close the distance. Take your opponent down, or pull them into your guard, so the fight happens on the ground where leverage matters more than strength.

02 POSITION

Improve your hold. Pass the legs, climb to side control or mount, or take the back. Each step makes the next move easier.

03 SUBMIT

Finish with a choke or a joint lock. Once it is locked in, your opponent taps, the round ends, and nobody needs to throw a punch.
